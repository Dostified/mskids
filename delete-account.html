Account & Data Deletion Request

Request permanent deletion or revocation of your MS Kids parent account and associated data.
🔑 Direct Google Account Revocation: Because MS Kids uses Google Sign-In, you have instant, 100% control to revoke app permissions directly from your Google Account at any time.

How to Revoke Access & Delete Your Data

Option 1: Direct Google Account Revocation (Instant)

You can instantly revoke MS Kids access directly from your Google Account settings:

  • Open myaccount.google.com/connections in any browser.
  • Select MS Kids under Third-party apps & services.
  • Click Delete all connections / Remove access to instantly revoke sign-in permissions.

Option 2: Direct In-App Account Sign Out & Wipe

  • Open the MS Kids app on your Android device.
  • Go to Settings ⚙️ → User Profile Card.
  • Tap Sign Out to disconnect your Google account and wipe local cache.

What Data is Deleted?

When an account deletion request is processed or revoked:

  • Your Parent Profile (Name, Avatar, Image Path, PIN, Pattern, Security Answers).
  • All Child Profiles associated with your parent account.
  • App usage statistics, challenge quiz histories, and timer configuration records.
  • Firebase Authentication tokens and Firestore documents (users/{userId}).

Data Retention Policy

We do not retain any user or child data after account deletion. All cloud records are erased immediately upon processing.
