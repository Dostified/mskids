Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026
🔒 Zero Data Selling Guarantee: MS Kids strictly DOES NOT sell, trade, or share family or child personal data with 3rd-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

1. Overview & Commitment

MS Kids ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and safety of families and children. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application collects, uses, and safeguards information when parents and children use the MS Kids Parental Control and Learning Application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Parent Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your Google Account email address and display name strictly for account identification and private cloud backup.
  • Family Profiles Data: Parent and child profile names, ages, classes, avatars, and profile photos configured by parents.
  • App Usage Statistics: Daily foreground app usage durations, challenge quiz scores, and daily time limits generated on your device.

3. Prominent System Permissions Disclosure

MS Kids requires specific Android system permissions to deliver parental control features:

  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used exclusively to detect active foreground applications and calculate daily time limits.
  • System Overlay (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used exclusively to display time's up interruption screens over restricted apps when time limits expire.
  • Device Admin (BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN): An optional feature used exclusively to prevent unauthorized uninstallation of MS Kids by children.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

MS Kids is built specifically for parents to protect and educate children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies:

  • We do not track or profile children for behavioral or targeted advertising.
  • We do not collect personal contact information directly from children.
  • Parents maintain 100% control over child profile creation, modification, and deletion.

5. Private Cloud Storage & Security

All family profiles, app restriction rules, and challenge histories are securely transmitted and stored in your private Firebase Firestore cloud document (users/{userId}) using encrypted TLS connections. Access is restricted to your authenticated account.

6. Account & Data Deletion Rights

Parents have the right to delete child profiles or their entire parent account data at any time directly within MS Kids settings or by requesting account deletion.
